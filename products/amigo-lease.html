01 / The Problem

A room is easy to find. A good roommate isn't.

Sharing a home is one of the most practical ways for young professionals and students to afford rent in neighbourhoods like East Legon and Osu. But listings only describe the room: price, location, amenities. They say nothing about the person you will share a kitchen with.

Most roommate conflict comes from everyday habits that nobody discusses up front: sleep schedules, guests, noise, tidiness, smoking, pets and who works from home.

Listings hide the lifestyle

A room post tells you about AC and distance to shops, not whether the other person blends smoothies at 6 AM while you're falling asleep.

Awkward conversations

Expectations about guests, cleaning rosters and quiet hours are hard to raise with a stranger, so they don't get raised until there is a problem.

Cold outreach

Seekers message hosts across WhatsApp groups and social posts with no context about whether they would actually get along.

02 / The Solution

Match on how people live, not just where

Amigo Lease builds a compatibility profile from a short lifestyle quiz and places each person into a friendly roommate archetype. Matches and listings are then ranked by compatibility, and people can chat in-app before meeting.

Archetypes are a conversation starter, not a box: they give two strangers shared language for expectations. The exact quiz scores stay private and are only used to calculate compatibility and flag potential friction points.

05 / Key Features

What Amigo Lease offers

Compatibility profile

Built from quiz answers on schedule, cleanliness, guests, noise, smoking, pets and work routine.

Eight archetypes

Calm Minimalist, Chill Creative, Cool Independent, Easygoing Socialite, Neat Planner, Night Owl Thinker, Quiet Professional, Warm Host.

Listings for hosts

Create and manage rooms with photos, price, amenities and house rules.

Matches & favourites

Roommate matches ranked by lifestyle compatibility; save listings for later.

Private chat

Conversations between seekers and hosts, started from a profile or listing.

Clear boundaries

Amigo Lease is a matching and discovery platform. It does not process rent; agreements stay between host and seeker.
