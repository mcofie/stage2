01 / The Urban Friction

The Advance Rent Crisis in Accra

In Ghana's capital, landlords routinely demand 1 to 2 years of advance rent upfront in a single lump sum, despite the statutory provisions of the Rent Act 1963 (Act 220). For early-career software developers, bankers, creative directors, and remote workers earning monthly salaries between GH₵ 5,000 and GH₵ 20,000, paying GH₵ 80,000 to GH₵ 180,000 upfront for a studio or 1-bedroom flat in Cantonments, Osu, or East Legon is virtually impossible.

Room-sharing and co-living would mathematically resolve this math—turning a luxury 3-bedroom apartment into affordable monthly per-person obligations. But in practice, roommate searching in Accra is hindered by a profound trust deficit: fear of stranger theft, non-payment of electricity (ECG prepayment tokens), lifestyle friction, and ghosting.

Amigo Lease is built to solve this mutual trust deficit by combining verified identity credentials with lifestyle compatibility algorithms and split-rent escrow protection.

02 / Field Research & Data

Interviews with 120 Urban Renters

Our product design team conducted detailed field interviews across 120 young professionals and 15 property brokers in Accra:

Insight #1: The Trust Deficit Outweighs Price

89% of participants stated they would prefer to share an apartment to save 50%+ on rent, but 74% refused to look on Twitter or classifieds because "you cannot verify who you are moving in with or whether they have a criminal record."

Insight #2: Micro-Frictions (Utilities & House Rules)

Among those who had previously shared flats, 68% of roommate breakups were triggered by unequal electricity and water bill distribution, unannounced long-term overnight guests, and conflicting remote work noise schedules.

Insight #3: Sub-lease Legal Ambiguity

Standard head-tenancy agreements in Ghana prohibit unauthorized subletting without landlord consent. Most shared arrangements exist informally without legally enforceable contracts, exposing tenants to sudden eviction.

03 / Platform Architecture

Verified Trust Infrastructure & Co-Tenancy

Amigo Lease transforms unverified social listings into a structured peer-matching market:

Multi-Tier Identity Verification

Mandatory government ID validation (Ghana Card) cross-referenced with LinkedIn employment history, social presence, and emergency contact verification.

Lifestyle Matrix Compatibility

Algorithmic matching across 18 living dimensions: work-from-home schedule, smoking/alcohol, sleep chronotype, cleanliness threshold, and guest policies.

Standard Co-Tenancy Contracts

Automated legally binding roommate agreements specifying rent contribution ratios, quiet hours, and formal dispute mediation protocols.

Split Utility & Escrow Ledger

Integrated micro-ledger tracking monthly ECG power, water, Wi-Fi, and cleaning fees, with automated Mobile Money reminders and split payments.
