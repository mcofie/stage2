01 / The Problem

Our best trips end up buried in a camera roll

People take hundreds of photos on a honeymoon, a big adventure or a weekend away, and then almost never look at them again. They sit between screenshots and receipts on a phone, or in a cloud folder no one opens.

Photo books fix that, but most tools feel like design software: dozens of templates, fiddly layouts, and uncertainty about whether a phone photo will actually print well.

Out of sight, out of mind

Digital photos don't get revisited, shared at the dinner table, or passed on.

Design is work

Laying out pages, picking fonts and arranging a story takes time and skill most people don't have.

Print anxiety

Low-resolution or cropped files often look fine on a phone and disappointing on paper.

02 / The Solution

A photobook workshop that does the hard parts

Itinero Goods is, in its own words, "a tiny photobook workshop + travel shop + itinerary lab." You pick the moments, choose a destination cover and add your own words. Layouts are ready-made but rearrangeable, and every page can be previewed before ordering.

Before anything is printed, a person checks the print file and gets in touch if a photo or page needs attention. Then the album is printed, bound and delivered.
