01 / The Automotive Aftermarket Friction

The Information Asymmetry of Car Repairs

Owning a car in Accra is fraught with distrust. The local aftermarket revolves around informal roadside workshops and the sprawling parts hub of Abossey Okai. While Ghanaian mechanics are exceptionally skilled at improvising repairs, the customer experience is broken: lack of diagnostic transparency, opaque parts markups, proliferation of dangerous counterfeit brake pads and suspension components, and zero unified vehicle service histories.

When car owners drop off their vehicle, they rarely receive itemized quotes or guarantees. When buying or selling a used vehicle, buyers cannot verify if oil changes were conducted on time or if the odometer was tampered with.

Motor Ambos transforms vehicle ownership into a transparent digital experience: digitized service passports, upfront parts pricing, mobile diagnostic bookings, and automated roadworthiness and insurance compliance renewals.

02 / Field Research & Workshop Audits

Field Audit Across 60 Drivers and 22 Garages

Our research team spent 6 weeks embedded in automotive workshops across Kokomlemle, Dansoman, and Spintex Road:

Discovery #1: The Counterfeit Spare Parts Threat

Over 65% of surveyed car owners unknowingly had counterfeit or degraded refurbished parts installed (e.g. spark plugs, ball joints) marketed as "original OEM," leading to recurring breakdowns within 90 days.

Discovery #2: Overbilling & "Trial-and-Error" Mechanics

84% of drivers reported paying for diagnostic "guesses" where mechanics replaced parts that were not faulty without solving the root check-engine code.

Discovery #3: The Secondary Market Penalty

Used vehicles in Ghana suffer an arbitrary 25–40% price discount on resale solely because sellers have zero verifiable paper trail or digital records proving maintenance rigor.

03 / Platform Architecture

Digital Passport & Escrow Repair Workflow

Motor Ambos separates diagnosis, parts procurement, and mechanic labor to eliminate misaligned incentives:

Immutable Digital Service Passport

Every maintenance event, part serial number, OBD-II diagnostic report, and mileage reading is cryptographically logged against the vehicle's chassis VIN.

Genuine Parts Escrow Rail

Parts are sourced directly from certified Tier-1 distributors with QR verification seals. Funds for labor are held in escrow and released only upon driver road-test approval.

Standardized Diagnostic Telemetry

Mechanics upload standard OBD-II trouble codes (DTCs). Motor Ambos auto-generates transparent breakdown explanations for the driver with fixed labor tariffs.

Statutory Compliance Reminders

Automated alerts for DVLA Roadworthy certificate renewals, insurance expiries, and scheduled transmission fluid changes via WhatsApp bots.
