01 / The Problem

Two moments every driver dreads

Car ownership in Ghana comes with recurring paperwork: insurance, roadworthy certificates and licences, each with its own expiry date. They are easy to forget until a police check or an accident makes them urgent.

The other moment is a breakdown. A flat battery or a dead engine on the roadside usually means calling around for "a guy," with no idea who is close, who is reliable or what it will cost.

Expired papers

Renewal dates for insurance, roadworthy and licence slip by because nothing reminds you in time.

Stranded and guessing

When the car breaks down, drivers don't know who is nearby or what the call-out fee will be until after they call.

Fleets on spreadsheets

Operators track papers and issues for every vehicle and driver by hand.

Mechanics hard to find

Good independent mechanics have no simple way to be found by drivers nearby.

02 / The Solution

Papers, reminders and roadside help in one app

Motor Ambos gives every car a digital glovebox: papers, service and fuel logs, and text-message reminders before anything expires. When something goes wrong, the same app shares your location and lists nearby providers by distance and call-out fee.

A QR sticker on the car makes help reachable even when you can't think straight: scanning it opens roadside help with the car's details already filled in, with a printed phone number as backup.
