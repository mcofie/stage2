01 / The Problem

When there is no will, families are left to guess

Most people put off a will because they assume it is only for the wealthy, or for people who own a house. In practice, the cost of not having one lands on the family. Accounts freeze, relatives disagree about what you would have wanted, and courts make decisions that should have been yours.

Modern lives also carry a layer older estate planning ignores: mobile money wallets, online accounts, photos and passwords that can be lost for good if no one is given the keys.

Frozen money

Bank and mobile money accounts can stay locked until the court grants letters of administration, which can take months.

Partners left exposed

Even a spouse must share what you leave with your parents and wider family, and a partner you have not married may get nothing.

Land and house disputes

Family houses and land are a common source of disputes, and widows and children can end up pushed out.

Children and pets

Your family or a court may have to decide who raises your children, and no one is named to take your pets in.

Lost digital life

Online accounts and photos can be locked away for good, and relatives can end up arguing over your funeral at the hardest time.

02 / The Solution

Your wishes, all in one place

Vellum replaces the intimidating lawyer's-office first step with a short, plain-language conversation. It asks about your life (partner, children, property, pets), shows you what would happen without a will, and then guides you to put your wishes in writing, one simple step at a time.

The message is deliberate: you don't need a house or savings to make a will. It is not about expecting the worst, it is about making sure everyone knows exactly what you would want.

03 / How It Works

A guided intake instead of a blank legal form

Vellum Onboarding Flow Mobile-first
01
Answer a few life questions
Do you have a partner? Children? A house or land, including one you are still building? Any pets?
02
Choose what matters most
Multi-select priorities: savings and mobile money, online accounts and photos, who looks after the children, who cares for the pets.
03
See "the honest truth"
A personalised side-by-side of what happens without a will versus with one, based only on your answers.
04
Share where to send next steps
A phone number (with a WhatsApp option). Answers are shared privately with the Vellum team.
05
Put your wishes in writing
The team guides you through drafting the will, digital asset plan and final instructions.
