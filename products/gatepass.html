01 / The Problem

Great events, sold with tools that don't fit

Events in Accra and across the continent are mostly promoted with static flyers on Instagram and WhatsApp, then sold through a patchwork of payment links, bank transfers and spreadsheets. The flyer can't show what the night actually feels like, and the checkout often doesn't match how people pay.

For organizers, every market brings a different payment provider, SMS gateway and currency, and it is hard to tell which promotion actually sold tickets.

Flyers don't sell the experience

A static poster says when and where, not the crowd, the sound or the energy that makes someone commit.

Full price, upfront

Premium tickets for conferences and festivals are a big one-off spend, so many people wait or never buy.

Fragmented payments

Mobile money in Ghana, M-Pesa in East Africa, cards abroad: organizers juggle providers or lose buyers at checkout.

No feedback loop

Without proper tracking, organizers can't see which ad or post drove purchases.

02 / The Solution

Discover through video, book in seconds

GatePass leads with video so an event can show its atmosphere, then makes buying a ticket a short, mobile-friendly flow. A built-in part-pay option lets attendees reserve a ticket today and finish paying over time.

Behind the storefront, organizers can use GatePass's own payments with no keys needed, or connect the payment, SMS and email providers that already work in their market.

05 / Key Features

Built for organizers and attendees

Video discovery

Event pages lead with video so people can feel the event before they buy.

Part-payment plans

Instalments per ticket type, with clear terms when a payment is missed.

Pan-African payments

Mobile money and cards in Ghana, M-Pesa in East Africa, Telebirr in Ethiopia, plus PayPal and Stripe for international buyers.

Fast payouts

Use GatePass payments with no setup and receive sales in your bank or mobile money the next business day.

Attendee messaging

Tickets by email, with SMS and email delivered through providers that work locally.

Ticket controls

Promo codes, add-ons, attendee questions, virtual tickets and ad conversion tracking.
