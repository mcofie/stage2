01 / The Discovery Problem

Beyond Static Event Flyers

West Africa is the epicenter of global Afrobeats culture, live festivals, and vibrant pop-ups. Yet ticketing in Accra and Lagos was stuck in 2012: grainy 1080x1080 JPEG flyers shared on Instagram Stories, confusing Google Forms, static PDF barcodes easily duplicated via WhatsApp screenshots, and massive 45-minute bottlenecks at event entrances.

Flyers fail to capture the energy, crowd, sound, and vibe of an event. Meanwhile, ticket fraud at high-profile concerts (Detty December in Ghana, Gidi Fest in Lagos) ran rampant, leaving fans locked outside while promoters suffered financial bleed.

GatePass replaces static flyers with an immersive, TikTok-style short-form video discovery feed where attending is one tap away, secured by cryptographic anti-fraud QR codes that function even during total cellular grid congestion.

02 / Field Research & Festival Audits

Entrance Audits Across 8 Major Festivals

During the December 2024–January 2025 concert season in Accra, our engineers conducted on-site telemetry across 8 events:

Discovery Friction: The Instagram Drop-off

81% of concertgoers discovered events through video recaps from creators rather than the promoter's graphic flyer. However, the path from watching an exciting video clip to securing a ticket averaged 5 friction-filled redirects.

Gate Chaos: Cellular Congestion Blackouts

When 5,000 to 15,000 attendees gather at open-air beachfronts (Labadi Beach, Untamed Empire), cellular towers experience severe packet loss. Cloud-only ticket scanners freeze, forcing security to abandon digital checks and rely on visual paper wristbands.

The "Screenshot" Scalping Black Market

Over 14% of entry disputes involved duplicate static PDF barcodes sold multiple times by scalpers outside the gates.

03 / Technical Architecture

Cryptographic Anti-Fraud & Local Mesh Scanning

GatePass combines consumer video streaming with mission-critical event operations:

Video Discovery Feed

HLS video streaming optimized for low-bandwidth mobile connections, featuring synchronized 1-click Mobile Money checkout overlays.

TOTP Dynamic QR Tickets

Tickets generate cryptographic time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) cycling every 15 seconds. Screenshots sent via WhatsApp fail validation instantly.

Local Wi-Fi / Bluetooth Mesh

Gate scanners synchronize scanned ticket digests peer-to-peer over local Bluetooth Low Energy and ad-hoc Wi-Fi, preventing double-entry with zero cellular internet.

Instant Settlement Rails

Direct integration with MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and global cards with real-time promoter revenue split dashboards.
