01 / The Human Friction

The Dating App Fatigue Epidemic

Online dating in African metropolitan hubs is broken. Tinder, Bumble, and Hinge suffer from extreme match inflation: users swipe endlessly, trade small-talk messages for weeks on WhatsApp, and rarely meet in person. For accomplished career professionals—lawyers, doctors, startup executives, and entrepreneurs—public dating apps carry heavy social stigma, privacy risks, and low signal-to-noise ratios.

Meanwhile, traditional social venues in Accra (loud nightclubs, crowded lounges) are hostile to meaningful conversation due to overpowering decibels, posturing, and unclear relationship intentionality.

Minutes 2 Match reconstructs romance as a high-trust, curated salon: vetted cohorts, structured 7-minute rotations, intentional icebreaker prompts, and a discreet web scorecard that reveals mutual interest only after the evening concludes.

02 / Field Research & Dating Psychology

Insights from 14 Live Cohorts

Stage 2 Labs designed and operated 14 sold-out matchmaking evenings across premium boutique venues in Accra:

Insight #1: The 7-Minute Chemistry Horizon

Field observations proved that human romantic chemistry, micro-expressions, posture, and voice timbre are subconsciously evaluated within the first 180 seconds. 7 minutes proved the optimal duration: long enough to discover mutual values, yet short enough to prevent awkward pauses if chemistry is absent.

Insight #2: Psychological Safety via Silent Voting

94% of female participants noted that in-person rejections or aggressive contact number requests at bars are uncomfortable. By eliminating phone number exchanges during the event and using private digital scorecards, pressure is entirely removed.

Insight #3: Cohort Vetting is Critical

The single highest predictor of event satisfaction was cohort alignment: balancing life stages, intellectual curiosity, and intentionality (e.g., long-term relationships vs. casual dating).

03 / Platform Architecture

Discreet Live Web Scorecard & Matching Engine

Minutes 2 Match pairs intimate hospitality with real-time graph algorithms:

Cohort Curation Algorithm

Pre-event screening questionnaire filtering for verified identity, career trajectory, relationship goals, and personal interests to guarantee balanced demographic cohorts.

Real-Time Table Web App

Each attendee receives a personal private web dashboard keyed to their seat number. At the ring of each bell, users tap "Pass", "Friend", or "Match" with private notes.

Mutual Consensus Resolution

At midnight following the event, the matching engine resolves mutual pairings. Contact details are shared only when both parties reciprocate interest.

Post-Event Concierge

Integrated date-planning concierge providing matched pairs with curated dinner reservations and exclusive partner venue discounts.
